TEACHING

My teaching focuses on helping students and practitioners understand complex problems through systems thinking, dynamic modelling and quantitative analysis.

I aim to connect conceptual understanding with hands-on analysis, encouraging learners to explore feedback, delays, unintended consequences and the long-term effects of decisions in complex systems.
